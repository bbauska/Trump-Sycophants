U.S. President Donald Trump signed Executive Order 14347 on September 5, 2025, to authorize the secondary title "Department of War" for the U.S. Department of Defense in non-statutory communications

## Legal Status: 
The order permits the department and its leadership to use the "Department of War" and "Secretary of War" as secondary titles in public materials, branding, and non-statutory correspondence. However, the official legal name remains the <em><b>Department of Defense</b></em> because changing a federal department's name permanently requires an act of <em><b>Congress</b></em>.
